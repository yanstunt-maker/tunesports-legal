Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use TuneSports?

No. TuneSports works fully offline with no sign-up. You only need an account if you want cloud backup so your routines and music are saved and can be restored on another device.

How do I back up or restore my routines?

Sign in to your account and open the Cloud Backup screen. Your routines, athletes, and music are backed up automatically. On a new or borrowed phone, sign in and choose Restore to bring everything back.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Subscription screen in the app and tap Manage Subscription, or go to Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the Subscription screen, tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and cloud data. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in your App Store settings first.

What disciplines does TuneSports support?

It's built for rhythmic gymnastics, figure skating, cheer, dance, ballroom, and similar performance sports — anywhere routines are set to music.

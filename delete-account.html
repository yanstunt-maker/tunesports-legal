TuneSports — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your TuneSports account and associated data. TuneSports is operated by United Stunts Inc.

How to delete your account in the app

You can delete your TuneSports account and all associated cloud data directly within the app:

  1. Open the TuneSports app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, your account and all associated backed-up data are permanently removed.

How to request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account and data deletion by emailing us at support@tune-sports.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and confirm once complete.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently delete:

  • Your account information (email address and hashed password).
  • All content backed up to the cloud (athlete names, profile photos, routine details, music/audio files, and cue points).
  • Your subscription identifier and associated purchase records held by us.

What data is kept

  • Content stored only on your device (if you never enabled cloud backup) is not held by us and is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Certain records may be retained where required by law, or by our payment providers (Apple App Store or Google Play) for their own billing and tax purposes. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data retention period

Account and cloud backup data are deleted promptly upon a confirmed deletion request. Any residual copies in routine backups are removed within 30 days.
